# GlobalShop 政策中心 · Policies

## refund | Refund & Return Policy 退换货政策
- 30 天无理由退货（需保持商品完好）。30-day free return; the item must be unused and in its original condition.
- 电子产品（如耳机、手表）一旦拆封，不支持无理由退货，除非有质量问题。Opened electronics (e.g. headphones, watches) cannot be returned unless there is a quality issue.
- 退货运费：质量问题卖家承担；非质量问题买家承担。Return shipping: paid by the seller for quality issues, by the buyer otherwise.
- 退款时效：退货验收通过后 5–10 个工作日退回原支付渠道。Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after inspection.

## shipping | Shipping Time 物流时效
- 美国 (US): 3–7 个工作日 (Standard Shipping)。US: 3–7 business days (Standard Shipping).
- 欧洲 (EU): 5–10 个工作日。EU: 5–10 business days.
- 其他地区: 10–15 个工作日。Other regions: 10–15 business days.
- 注意：所有订单需 1–2 个工作日处理时间。All orders need 1–2 business days of processing before dispatch.

## customs | Customs & Duties 关税政策
- 美国订单：所有商品价格已包含关税 (DDP)。US orders: duties are included in the price (DDP).
- 欧洲订单：由于 VAT 新规，买家可能需要自行缴纳部分增值税。EU orders: under the new VAT rules, the buyer may need to pay part of the VAT on delivery.
- 我们不会通过非官方链接收取关税。We never collect duties through unofficial links.

## tracking | Order Tracking 物流追踪
- 使用订单号在官网查询物流。Track your parcel on our website with your order number.
- 物流信息每 6–12 小时更新一次；7 天无更新可联系客服按丢件处理。Tracking updates every 6–12 hours; if there is no update for 7 days, contact support and we will treat it as a lost parcel.

## payment | Payment & Currency 支付与币种
- 支持 Visa、Mastercard、American Express、PayPal、Apple Pay、Google Pay。We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- 商品以美元 (USD) 计价并扣款。Prices are charged in USD.
- 我们不会通过邮件索要 CVV 或密码。We never ask for your CVV or password by email.

## warranty | Warranty 保修
- 电子产品自签收起提供 12 个月有限保修，覆盖制造缺陷，不含人为或进水损坏。Electronics carry a 12-month limited warranty from delivery, covering manufacturing defects only (not accidental or water damage).
- 保修申请：提供订单号、照片或视频、问题描述。For a claim: order number, photo/video and a description of the issue.

## contact | Contact 联系方式
- 在线客服：AI 7×24 小时，人工 09:00–21:00（UTC+8）。Live chat: AI 24/7, human agents 09:00–21:00 (UTC+8).
- 邮箱：support@globalshop.example，24 小时内回复。Email: support@globalshop.example, replies within 24 hours.

## complaints | Complaints 投诉
- 首次响应 4 个工作小时内，48 小时内给出解决方案。First response within 4 business hours; resolution target 48 hours.
- 补偿阶梯：退运费 → 部分退款 10–30% → 全额退款或补发。Compensation ladder: shipping-fee refund → 10–30% partial refund → full refund or replacement.
